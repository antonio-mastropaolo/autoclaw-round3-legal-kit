# Method Card — University Cloud-AI Vendor Review

**One-page reusable method.** Fork this card for any public-institution (or similarly constrained) review of a cloud AI-agent or research SaaS vendor.  
**Demo packet:** Coastal Commonwealth University / Kelworth Lab vs AgentForge Cloud (synthetic).  
**Not legal advice.** This is a workflow, not an opinion on any real vendor or institution.

---

## When to use

Counsel, procurement, or a research unit has received a vendor's **MSA + DPA + SLA** (and maybe an order form) and must decide, on a short clock, whether a university may proceed to a purchase order, and on what conditions. The service will hold research content and may touch student or collaborator data. A single-document redline is not enough: the documents will conflict, and a "comfort" sentence in one will be undone in another.

## Inputs (ingest only these)

| Give the legal assistant | Do not give the legal assistant |
| :--- | :--- |
| Matter / intake brief (parties, data types, must-haves, timeline) | **The private answer key (pre-registered issue set)** |
| Vendor MSA, DPA (with annexes), SLA, and any order form | Marketing decks, "we'll take care of that" emails, or security white papers offered *in lieu of* contract text |
| The institution's AI-procurement playbook or checklist | Real client, student, patient, or employee data; unredacted live contracts |

**Explicit rule: do not upload answer keys, scoring rubrics, or planned-issue lists to the agent.** The assistant must issue-spot from the paper. The answer key is used only *after* the run, by a human, to score what was caught, missed, or invented.

## Workflow (do these in order)

1. **Intake.** Identify the contracting party (the institution, not the lab or the PI). Classify data (HIPAA? FERPA-possible? export? research IP?). Lock must-haves that the unit may not waive. Note the PO clock and the authorizing official.
2. **Ingest MSA + DPA + SLA + playbook.** Upload the four (or five, with the brief) source files and nothing else. Record the exact file set and versions.
3. **Issue spot.** Require pinpoint section cites. Force a fact / inference split: quote or cite the clause, then state the counsel judgment separately. Never invent statutes, agency guidance, or clause numbers.
4. **Cross-document contradiction map.** Build a matrix with a row per conflict and columns for MSA cite, DPA cite, SLA cite, playbook requirement, resolution / redline ask, and status. Minimum expected in this demo family: training vs "personal data only"; handover vs deletion; credits as sole remedy vs DPA / security duties; subprocessor notice periods; breach-notice clocks; plus the **order-of-precedence overlay** that can be used to argue every other conflict away. A finding that cites only one document is incomplete.
5. **Word redlines.** Produce tracked-change `.docx` mark-ups (or a clause-by-clause equivalent ready to drop into Word), prioritized: P0 first, then P1, then P2. Redlines must implement the contradiction resolutions, not merely comment "this is vendor-favorable."
6. **Scorecard vs the private answer key.** *After* the agent run, and only on the human side, compare claims to the pre-authored issue register. Record caught / partial / missed / false positive against a fixed denominator. Do not show the key to the agent and do not "grade on a curve" by editing the key after the fact.
7. **Human verification ledger.** For each pre-registered issue (and each extra AutoClaw claim): what the agent claimed, the cite it gave, Y / N / Partial, and any correction. Outstanding-grade work documents at least one real corrected error if one occurred — never invent an error, and never leave the ledger blank and call the run "verified."
8. **Conditions of award.** Translate the verified issues into a P0 / P1 / P2 table a general counsel or VP for Research can forward: do-not-proceed items, same-markup negotiates, raise-and-document items, open questions, and a clear proceed / proceed-only-if / do-not-proceed disposition. This intake still does not authorize signature.

## Output pack

- Tracked-change Word redlines (MSA, DPA, SLA)  
- Cited risk memo (facts vs inferences labeled)  
- Contradiction matrix (with order-of-precedence row)  
- Conditions-of-award table (P0 / P1 / P2)  
- Verification ledger + scorecard (human-completed; answer key stays private)  
- Open questions for the vendor and the sponsoring unit  

## Quality bar (and common failure modes)

- Cites are section numbers, not "the liability cap is too low."  
- Map every material issue to the institutional checklist, including public-entity constraints (sovereign immunity, venue, indemnity authority, appropriations / auto-renewal, FOIA) and FERPA if education records are possible.  
- Do not stop at a DPA "no training" sentence if the MSA license, definitions, precedence clause, or surviving IP grant say otherwise.  
- Credits may be an exclusive remedy for ordinary downtime; they must not swallow security or breach claims.  
- Format is part of the work: real Word revisions and a one-page conditions table, not a markdown wall.  
- Reuse this card; rewrite the brief and playbook for the next institution. Never reuse a live vendor's paper or an unredacted matter file as a demo.
