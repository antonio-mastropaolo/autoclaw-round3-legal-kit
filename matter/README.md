## Notice to Readers

All materials in this folder are **synthetic fiction** prepared solely to demonstrate a university-counsel workflow for AutoClaw Round 3 (Legal Practice). They are **not legal advice**, not a formbook, and not a description of any real transaction, investigation, or dispute.

No document in this packet is an agreement of Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic, Z.ai, AutoClaw, any real university, or any real vendor. Party names, product names, addresses, clause text, contact details, and exhibit references were invented for this exercise.

**Do not use these drafts as a substitute for advice from qualified counsel.** A real procurement would require review under the institution's actual policies, applicable law, and the vendor's then-current paper.

## Intended Use

These files are Word-ready Markdown sources (numbered sections, defined terms, exhibit lists). They may be converted to `.docx` for a redline exercise, uploaded to a legal-assistant workflow, or used to produce a risk memo, issue list, or negotiation playbook.

The vendor paper is intentionally uneven. Several positions are more favorable to the Provider than a public research university would typically accept, and a number of points conflict across the MSA, DPA, and SLA. Those features are there so a reviewer can practice issue-spotting, cross-document mapping, and reasoned mark-up — not because they represent a recommended market position.
