This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") forms part of the AgentForge Cloud Master Subscription Agreement (the "**Agreement**") between AgentForge Inc. In all other respects, the Agreement controls, including with respect to Customer Data that is not Customer Personal Data, Outputs, intellectual-property licenses, limitation of liability, and term and termination.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1** "**Applicable Data Protection Law**" means, to the extent applicable to the processing of Customer Personal Data under the Agreement, the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("**GDPR**"), the United Kingdom GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("**CCPA**"), and any similar law governing the processing of personal data that applies to a Party in connection with the Service. For the avoidance of doubt, this DPA does not itself determine whether any particular law applies to Customer.

**1.2** "**Customer Personal Data**" means any personal data or personal information contained within Customer Data that Provider processes on behalf of Customer in the course of providing the Service. Customer Personal Data does not include Provider's account, billing, or prospect information processed as an independent controller, or de-identified or aggregated data that is not personal data under Applicable Data Protection Law.

**1.3** "**Personal Data Breach**" means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data in Provider's possession or control, as those concepts are used in Applicable Data Protection Law.

**1.4** "**SCCs**" means the standard contractual clauses for the transfer of personal data to third countries pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2016/679, as adopted by the European Commission, to the extent applicable and properly completed.

**1.5** "**Subprocessor**" means a third party engaged by Provider to process Customer Personal Data in connection with the Service.

**1.6** The terms "**controller**," "**processor**," "**personal data**," "**process**," "**data subject**," and "**supervisory authority**" have the meanings given in the GDPR, and equivalent terms under other Applicable Data Protection Law shall be read mutatis mutandis. Under the CCPA, Provider acts as a "service provider" or "processor" with respect to Customer Personal Data, and the terms of this DPA shall be interpreted consistently with that status.

## 2. ROLES AND SCOPE

**2.1** The Parties acknowledge that, with respect to Customer Personal Data, Customer is the controller (or a processor instructing Provider on behalf of a third-party controller) and Provider is the processor (or subprocessor, as the case may be). If Customer is a processor, Customer warrants that its instructions, and Customer's engagement of Provider, are authorized by the relevant controller.

**2.2** This DPA applies only to Provider's processing of Customer Personal Data in providing the Service. It does not apply to processing for which Provider is an independent controller (including billing, fraud prevention with respect to Provider's own operations, and improvement of Provider's corporate website).

**2.3** The subject matter, nature, and purpose of the processing, the types of Customer Personal Data, and the categories of data subjects are described in Annex I. Customer is responsible for the accuracy of Annex I as it relates to Customer's actual use of the Service.

## 3. CUSTOMER INSTRUCTIONS

**3.1** Provider shall process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from Customer, unless required to do so by applicable law, in which case Provider shall, to the extent legally permitted, inform Customer of that legal requirement before processing.

**3.2** For purposes of this DPA, **"documented instructions"** means: (a) the processing described in the Agreement, this DPA, and the Documentation; (b) Customer's use of the Service's then-available administrative settings and features; and (c) written instructions that Customer and Provider expressly agree in a signed amendment or Order Form. **Provider is not obligated to comply with instructions that are outside the functionality of the Service, that would require a material change to Provider's architecture or subprocessors, or that in Provider's reasonable judgment violate applicable law or this DPA.** If Provider declines such an instruction, Customer may terminate the affected Service in accordance with the Agreement, without additional refund obligation beyond what the Agreement already provides.

**3.3** Customer shall not instruct Provider to process Customer Personal Data in a manner that would cause Provider to violate Applicable Data Protection Law. Customer is solely responsible for determining that the Service, as configured by Customer, is suitable for Customer's legal obligations, including any obligations under education-privacy, research, or employment laws.

## 4. USE LIMITATIONS; NO TRAINING ON CUSTOMER PERSONAL DATA

**4.1** Provider shall not sell Customer Personal Data, as "sale" is defined under the CCPA, or otherwise disclose Customer Personal Data except as permitted by this DPA and the Agreement.

**4.2** **Provider shall not use Customer Personal Data to train, fine-tune, or otherwise develop generalized machine-learning or foundation models, and shall not permit Subprocessors to do so**, except where Customer has provided a documented instruction under Section 3 that clearly authorizes such use for a specific, time-bounded project. This Section 4.2 does not restrict Provider from: (a) using Customer Personal Data to provide, maintain, and secure the Service to Customer, including retrieval-augmented generation confined to Customer's tenant; (b) creating de-identified or aggregated statistics that do not identify a data subject or Customer; or (c) using usage data that is not Customer Personal Data.

**4.3** Nothing in this Section 4 limits any license or right granted to Provider under the Agreement with respect to data that is not Customer Personal Data, including non-personal Customer Data, Outputs that do not constitute personal data, feedback, and usage data.

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND PERSONNEL

Provider shall ensure that persons authorized to process Customer Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality, and that they receive appropriate training in data protection.

## 6. SECURITY

**6.1** Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of the processing, Provider shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, as further described in Annex III. Provider may update those measures, provided that the updates do not materially diminish the overall level of protection.

**6.2** Upon written request, not more than once per twelve (12) month period (unless a Personal Data Breach has occurred), Provider shall make available to Customer a summary of its then-current SOC 2 Type II report, or an equivalent independent audit report, under a customary non-disclosure arrangement. Provider is not obligated to permit on-site inspections of its facilities except where required by Applicable Data Protection Law and after the Parties have reasonably exhausted alternative means of demonstration.

## 7. SUBPROCESSORS

**7.1** Customer generally authorizes Provider to engage Subprocessors to process Customer Personal Data, including the Subprocessors listed in Annex II as of the effective date of this DPA, and to engage new Subprocessors in accordance with this Section 7.

**7.2** Provider shall impose contractual obligations on each Subprocessor that are no less protective of Customer Personal Data in material respects than those in this DPA, to the extent applicable to the services provided by the Subprocessor. Provider remains responsible for each Subprocessor's performance of its data-protection obligations with respect to Customer Personal Data, subject to the limitations of liability in the Agreement.

**7.3** Provider shall maintain a current list of Subprocessors in Annex II or at a URL designated by Provider. Provider shall provide Customer with notice of the addition or replacement of a Subprocessor at least fifteen (15) days before the new Subprocessor begins processing Customer Personal Data (or as soon as reasonably practicable in the case of an emergency replacement). Customer may object to a new Subprocessor on reasonable data-protection grounds by written notice within ten (10) days of Provider's notice. If the Parties cannot resolve the objection, Customer's sole remedy is to terminate the affected Service before the new Subprocessor is engaged, with a refund of prepaid, unused Fees for the terminated portion of the then-current term. Customer's failure to object within the ten (10) day period constitutes authorization of the Subprocessor.

**7.4** The Subprocessors identified in Annex II include infrastructure, content-delivery, and **product-analytics** providers. Customer is responsible for reviewing that list against Customer's own policies, including any restrictions applicable to education records, research data, or international collaborators, before submitting such data to the Service.

## 8. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

**8.1** Customer acknowledges that Provider may process and transfer Customer Personal Data in the United States and in other countries in which Provider or its Subprocessors maintain operations.

**8.2** **Where a transfer of Customer Personal Data originating in the European Economic Area requires a transfer mechanism, the Parties agree that the SCCs, Module Two (controller to processor) or Module Three (processor to processor), as applicable, shall be deemed incorporated by reference to the extent required by Applicable Data Protection Law.** The Parties shall complete any annexes or optional clauses of the SCCs as reasonably necessary. If there is a conflict between the SCCs and this DPA, the SCCs prevail as to the transferred EEA data.

**8.3** **Transfers of Customer Personal Data originating in the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or other jurisdictions that require a specific addendum or approved transfer tool will be addressed in a separate addendum if and when the Parties agree that such an addendum is required.** This DPA does not itself incorporate a UK International Data Transfer Addendum, a Swiss addendum, or any ASEAN or other regional transfer instrument. **Customer may request, and Provider may in its discretion offer, an optional transfer configuration for data hosted in the Republic of Singapore; that configuration is not part of the standard Service and is available only if expressly stated on an Order Form.**

**8.4** Provider does not warrant that the transfer mechanisms described in this Section 8 will be sufficient for every Customer use case, including transfers involving special-category data, education records, or data of minors, and Customer remains responsible for its own transfer-impact assessments.

## 9. ASSISTANCE; DATA-SUBJECT REQUESTS

**9.1** Taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to Provider, Provider shall provide Customer with reasonable assistance in responding to data-subject requests, in completing data-protection impact assessments, and in consultations with supervisory authorities, in each case solely with respect to Provider's processing of Customer Personal Data for the Service. Provider may charge reasonable professional-services fees for assistance that exceeds ordinary self-service features of the Service or that is not required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

**9.2** If Provider receives a request from a data subject relating to Customer Personal Data, Provider shall, to the extent legally permitted, direct the data subject to Customer and shall not respond substantively except as required by law or as authorized by Customer.

## 10. PERSONAL DATA BREACH

**10.1** **Provider shall notify Customer of a Personal Data Breach without undue delay after becoming aware of it.** Provider's notification shall describe, to the extent reasonably known at the time: (a) the nature of the Personal Data Breach, including the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned; (b) the likely consequences; (c) the measures taken or proposed to address the Personal Data Breach; and (d) a point of contact. Subsequent updates may be provided as information becomes available.

**10.2** Provider's notification of, or response to, a Personal Data Breach is not an acknowledgement of fault or liability. Customer is solely responsible for notifying regulators, data subjects, or other persons, except to the extent Applicable Data Protection Law imposes a direct notification duty on Provider, in which case the Parties shall reasonably coordinate.

**10.3** Notice under this Section 10 shall be sent to the security or privacy contact designated on the Order Form, or if none is designated, to Customer's primary administrative email.

## 11. RETURN AND DELETION

**11.1** **Upon termination or expiry of the Agreement, Provider shall, within ninety (90) days, delete Customer Personal Data from the production Service, or, if Customer submits a written request within thirty (30) days after termination or expiry, shall first make Customer Personal Data available for export in a commonly used machine-readable format then supported by the Service, and thereafter delete it within ninety (90) days after the end of that export window or after Customer's written confirmation, whichever is earlier.**

**11.2** Provider may retain Customer Personal Data to the extent required by applicable law, or in encrypted backups that are overwritten in the ordinary backup cycle (not to exceed an additional one hundred twenty (120) days after deletion from production), provided that such retained data remains subject to this DPA until deleted.

**11.3** Upon written request, Provider shall provide a written attestation of deletion from a responsible officer or security lead. Provider is not obligated to delete de-identified or aggregated data, usage data, or artifacts that are not Customer Personal Data.

**11.4** Customer acknowledges that the deletion schedule in this Section 11 is designed for ordinary off-boarding and **is not a managed transition, parallel-run, or professional handover service**. Any extended retention, staged migration, or transformation of Customer Data is outside this DPA and, if available at all, must be purchased separately under the Agreement before the deletion period begins.

## ANNEX I — DETAILS OF PROCESSING

**A. Subject matter and duration.** Processing of Customer Personal Data as necessary to provide, maintain, support, and secure the AgentForge Cloud Service, for the term of the Agreement plus the retention and deletion periods in Section 11.

**B. Nature and purpose.** Hosting, storage, transmission, display, backup, logging, user administration, support diagnostics, and generation of Outputs at Customer's direction.

**C. Types of Customer Personal Data (as determined by Customer).** Account identifiers (name, email, user ID); professional affiliation and role; authentication and usage logs; content that Authorized Users choose to submit to the Service, which may incidentally include personal data present in code comments, research notes, correspondence, or file metadata. Provider does not require special-category data or protected health information to provide the Service, and the Service is not designed for those data types.

**D. Categories of data subjects (as determined by Customer).** Customer's personnel, contractors, students, and invited collaborators who are Authorized Users, and any other individuals whose personal data Customer or an Authorized User elects to submit.

**E. Processing operations.** Collection, recording, organization, storage, retrieval, consultation, use, alignment, restriction, erasure, and disclosure to Subprocessors as described in Annex II.

## ANNEX III — TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES (SUMMARY)

Provider maintains, among other measures: (a) encryption of Customer Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and encryption at rest using industry-standard algorithms; (b) logical tenant isolation within the multi-tenant Service; (c) access control based on role and least privilege, with multi-factor authentication for Provider administrative access; (d) logging and monitoring of administrative access; (e) vulnerability management and periodic independent assessment; (f) business-continuity and backup procedures designed for the production Service; and (g) a written information-security program reviewed at least annually. This Annex III is a summary only and does not create a warranty that any particular incident will not occur.
